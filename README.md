Neon Sunset Cover Lab is an offline GTA 6 cover art maker: build multi-panel posters with bold lettering and sunset/neon grades, then export them as PNG. Pick a layout, drop in a photo, tune the grade and captions, and save a poster that looks straight out of Leonida. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Features

- 4 layouts: Hero + two, Three strips, 2x2 grid, Full bleed
- 4 colour grades: Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Noir
- Bold poster lettering with caption, subtitle, size, colour and position controls
- Click a photo to assign it, drag to pan, zoom from 1x to 2.5x
- Export PNG at 1080x1080, 1080x1350 or 1920x1080
- Works fully offline with 2 built-in sample photos
